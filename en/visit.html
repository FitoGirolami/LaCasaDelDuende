At dusk, the garden changes

During the day, visitors can observe the forms, materials and surrounding nature. As the first shadows arrive, lights appear among the paths and each figure reveals a different face.

During the visit

What you will find

  • Paths that you can explore freely.
  • Original sculptures of duendes, fairies and symbolic creatures.
  • Small texts and hidden details.
  • An evening light experience.
  • The original art shop of La Casa del Duende.

You can visit at your own pace. No reservation or rigid guided route is required.

Accessibility and comfort

The main entrance is accessible to wheelchairs, although some internal areas have gentle changes in level. Visitor restrooms, Wi-Fi in the main area and places to pause are available.

La Casa del Duende and La Aldea Mágica are independent artistic projects. Tickets are sold only at the venue; we do not work with street promoters in the city centre.
