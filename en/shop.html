Frequently asked questions

Are the figures unique pieces?

Yes. They are made one at a time, and every piece has its own features, expression and finish.

Yes. You can request current photographs and coordinate the purchase through WhatsApp.

Do you ship?

Shipping within Ecuador and to other countries can be arranged according to destination, size and transport conditions.
