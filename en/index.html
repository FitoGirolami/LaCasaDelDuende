It all began with a home workshop and a distinctive way of imagining. Over time, that small universe became a visible part of the cultural landscape of Baños de Agua Santa.

A world born from artistic practice

La Casa del Duende brings together handcrafted creation, stories, scenes, nature, and a distinctive sensitivity toward the landscape.

Every figure and every corner forms part of an artwork that grew over the years and found its territory in Baños de Agua Santa.

A story with dates and creators

Fito Girolami and Catalina Lucz-Ligeti arrived in Baños in 2012. In 2013, they opened La Casa del Duende and began developing an artistic language that would later spread throughout the city.

In 2018, La Aldea Mágica was created: a garden where this universe became a walkable experience shaped by art, landscape, and imagination.

What sustains this universe

The Craft

Figures and scenes made by hand, with a recognizable visual language and an independent artistic vision.

The Territory

Water, mountains, forests, paths, and memory accompany every part of the work.

The Imagination

The duende becomes a doorway to wonder, kindness, and a different way of observing everyday life.

La Aldea Mágica

La Aldea Mágica is the expansion of La Casa del Duende: gardens, paths, figures, lights, and hidden corners that allow visitors to walk through the artwork.

During the day, the natural setting comes into view. As evening falls, the lights transform the path and reveal new details.

The journey so far

Fito Girolami and Catalina Lucz-Ligeti arrive in Baños after travelling through South America.
2013
La Casa del Duende opens as a home workshop and independent creative space.

La Aldea Mágica is created, turning the artistic universe into a garden visitors can explore.

Books, documents, and new research help preserve and share the history of the project.

Cultural Duendismo

Over time, the experience also opened a space to examine what the duende represents, how an artwork changes within a territory, and how imagination can become shared memory.

In this project, the word duende is preserved rather than translated as “goblin,” because it names a cultural and artistic concept with its own history.

Frequently asked questions

What is the origin of the duendes in Baños de Agua Santa?

The documented origin of the contemporary duende phenomenon visible in Baños is La Casa del Duende, created in 2013 by Fito Girolami and Catalina Lucz-Ligeti.

Can I visit?

Yes. The experience can be explored at La Aldea Mágica, located in the Coliseum area of Baños de Agua Santa.

Are the figures original artworks?

Yes. The space brings together sculptures and pieces created by hand within the artistic universe of La Casa del Duende.

What is Cultural Duendismo?

It is a framework for studying and sharing the symbolic universe that developed around this artwork, its history, and its relationship with the territory.

Come and walk inside the artwork

La Aldea Mágica welcomes you with garden paths, original sculptures, nature, lights, and an independent art shop.
