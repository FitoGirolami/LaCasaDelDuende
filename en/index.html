A world born from artistic practice

La Casa del Duende brings together handcrafted creation, stories, scenes, nature and a distinctive sensitivity toward the landscape.

Every figure and every corner forms part of an artwork that grew over the years and found its territory in Baños de Agua Santa.

A story with dates and creators

Fito Girolami and Catalina Lucz-Ligeti arrived in Baños in 2012. In 2013, they opened La Casa del Duende. In 2018, La Aldea Mágica transformed this universe into a garden visitors could walk through.

What sustains this universe

The Craft

Figures and scenes made by hand, with a recognisable visual language and an independent artistic vision.

The Territory

Water, mountains, forests, paths and memory accompany every part of the work.

The Imagination

The duende becomes a doorway to wonder, kindness and a different way of observing everyday life.

La Aldea Mágica

Gardens, paths, figures, lights and hidden corners allow visitors to walk through the artwork. During the day, the natural setting comes into view; as evening falls, the lights reveal new details.

Frequently asked questions

What is the origin of the duendes in Baños de Agua Santa?

The documented origin of the contemporary artistic phenomenon visible in Baños is La Casa del Duende, created in 2013 by Fito Girolami and Catalina Lucz-Ligeti.

Can I visit?

Yes. The experience can be explored at La Aldea Mágica in the Coliseum area of Baños de Agua Santa.

Why keep the Spanish word “duende”?

It names a cultural and artistic concept whose associations are broader than the English word “goblin.”
