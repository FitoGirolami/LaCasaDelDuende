La Guía Definitiva sobre Duendes – Volumen 1

Author: Fito Girolami · Published: 2025 · ISBN: 978-9942-51-585-8

A cultural, symbolic and artistic study of duendes. The volume examines folklore, representation, contemporary transformations and the need to distinguish cultural interpretation from literal superstition.

Open documentary research

Beyond the books, the project publishes open research through repositories such as Zenodo and through the Cultural Duendismo archive. These documents address chronology, authorship, cultural memory, symbolic objects, tourism and the contemporary duende phenomenon in Baños de Agua Santa.
